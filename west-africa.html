Culinary region

West Africa

West African cooking is bold and generous. Meals are built on a base of tomato, onion and pepper, and are often cooked slowly in one pot to feed a crowd.

Staples

  • Rice, millet and sorghum
  • Yam, cassava and plantain
  • Black-eyed peas and groundnuts
  • Palm oil and leafy greens

Cooking techniques

  • One-pot rice cooking
  • Slow-simmered stews and soups
  • Pounding and shaping dishes like fufu
  • Grilling spiced meat over open fire

Spices and flavors

Heat and aroma come from scotch bonnet peppers, ginger, thyme and grains of selim.
