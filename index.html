Latest food trends

What is changing in African kitchens and restaurants.

Ancient grains return

Cooks and restaurants are rediscovering teff, fonio and sorghum for their flavor and nutrition.

Plant-forward traditions

Leafy stews, beans and groundnut sauces have long been plant-rich, and they are gaining new fans worldwide.

Street food goes global

Dishes like bunny chow and suya are moving from market stalls to restaurant menus around the world.
