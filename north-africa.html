Culinary region

North Africa

North African food blends sweet, sour and savory. Spice routes and Mediterranean ingredients shaped dishes that are slow-cooked, fragrant and made for sharing.

Staples

  • Couscous and wheat bread
  • Olives and olive oil
  • Chickpeas and lentils
  • Dates, nuts and preserved lemons

Cooking techniques

  • Slow braising in a clay tagine pot
  • Steaming couscous over a stew
  • Grilling and roasting
  • Preserving lemons in salt

Spices and flavors

Warmth comes from cumin, cinnamon, saffron, harissa and ras el hanout.
